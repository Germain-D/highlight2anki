The long way into a language

Culture · 12 min read

Anyone learning a new language collects words like pebbles. Some are left behind, others resurface weeks later, in a sentence you never saw coming.

That serendipity is exactly what makes reading the best vocabulary drill there is: the word arrives with its sentence, its tone, the paragraph it stood in.

Write it down right away, instead of looking it up painfully later, and it almost always sticks.
